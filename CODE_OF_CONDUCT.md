# Code of Conduct

Murmura follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for the effect of your words and actions.
- Keep technical discussions focused on evidence, reproducible behavior, and project goals.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports will be reviewed with care and handled as confidentially as possible.
